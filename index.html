About

Research profile

Poultry nutrition and health researcher specializing in intestinal integrity, skeletal biology, mycotoxicology, and the gut microbiome. I develop nutritional and management strategies to strengthen bird health, welfare, productivity, and resilience to disease and environmental stress.

I pair gut-permeability assays, qPCR, ELISA, and flow cytometry with DEXA and micro-CT imaging of bone and body composition — connecting bench-level mechanism to production-relevant welfare and management outcomes.
